What is TTR?

Token Type Ratio (TTR) is a simple but powerful measure of lexical diversity, often used in linguistics and computational text analysis. It quantifies how varied the vocabulary is in a text.

Formula:
TTR = Number of unique words ÷ Total number of words

For example, if a sentence has 10 words and 8 of them are unique, its TTR is 0.8. A TTR of 1.0 means every word is unique; lower values indicate repetition.

TTR is sensitive to text length — longer texts naturally tend to have lower TTR values. That’s why this app supports both:

  • Cumulative TTR: Lexical diversity as the text grows.
  • Rolling TTR: TTR computed in fixed-size moving windows, revealing local variation.
