What is TTR?

Token Type Ratio (TTR) measures lexical diversity — how rich or repetitive a vocabulary is. It is calculated as:

TTR = Number of unique words ÷ Total number of words

Higher TTR means more varied vocabulary. Lower TTR indicates more repetition. Since TTR declines with longer texts, this app lets you analyze:

  • Cumulative TTR: From start to finish.
  • Rolling TTR: Sliding window view for local diversity.
